List of selected major events that Dr Yizengaw participated in organizing or co-corganized

The 15th International Symposium on Equatorial Aeronomy (ISEA) conference in India during 19-23 October 2018 is coming soon.

The 14th International Symposium on Equatorial Aeronomy (ISEA) conference has been held successfully in Bahir Dar, Ethiopia during 19-23 October 2015. See Final Report also produced a special issue at the Annales Geophysicae

The AGU Chapman conference on longitudinal and hemispheric dependence of space weather, which was the first space weather Chapman conference in the African continent, has been held successfully in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia during 12-16 November 2012. See Final Report . The confrence also produced a book

A three day workshop held at Boston College during 1-3 March 2012 to address the concept of an Advanced Modular Incoherent Scatter Radar (AMISR) in Ethiopia. See Final Report .

The second IHY-Africa was held in Livingstone, Zambia during 7-12 June 2009. See Final Report .

The first IHY-Africa (may be the first international space science conference) has been held in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia during 12-16 November 2007. See Final Report .
